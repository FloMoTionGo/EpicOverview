Westeros, step 1 of 7

Advance the hold counters

All at once. At the board, before any card is drawn.

Turn the die next to each influence track: one up if the same house still holds the token as last round, back to 1 if it changed hands.

A die reaches 4: That house can win at the end of the round: Throne with King's Landing, Blade with the most castles, or Raven with the most Power.
Round 1: The whole Westeros phase is skipped.
The dice live on the board. The app does not know who holds Throne, Blade or Raven.

Source: EPIC p. 6–7
